# Stress Management

## Overview
Stress occurs when demands exceed available resources. Long-term stress can affect mood, sleep, and physical health. This guide outlines practical ways to reduce stress and build resilience.

## Key Strategies
- **Daily planning:** Break tasks into smaller steps and prioritize essentials.
- **Time boundaries:** Set limits on work hours, screen time, and commitments.
- **Journaling:** Writing thoughts down helps organize emotions and identify patterns.
- **Relaxation routines:** Stretching, meditation, or quiet time can reset the body.
- **Healthy habits:** Hydration, balanced meals, and regular movement support stress recovery.

## Signs Stress Is Becoming Unmanageable
- Feeling constantly overwhelmed  
- Difficulty sleeping or waking up tired  
- Irritability or emotional outbursts  
- Trouble concentrating  
- Physical symptoms like headaches or muscle tension
