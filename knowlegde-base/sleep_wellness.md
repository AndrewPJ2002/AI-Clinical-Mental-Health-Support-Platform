# Sleep & Wellness

## Overview
Sleep plays a critical role in emotional stability, cognitive function, and physical health. Poor sleep can worsen anxiety, stress, and mood disorders. This guide provides strategies to improve sleep quality.

## Key Strategies
- **Consistent schedule:** Go to bed and wake up at the same time daily.
- **Wind-down routine:** Create a calming pre-sleep ritual (reading, stretching, dim lights).
- **Limit stimulants:** Reduce caffeine, heavy meals, and screen exposure before bed.
- **Sleep environment:** Keep the room cool, dark, and quiet.
- **Mind-body techniques:** Gentle breathing or progressive muscle relaxation can ease tension.

## When Sleep Problems Need Attention
- Difficulty falling asleep most nights  
- Waking up frequently or too early  
- Feeling unrested despite adequate hours  
- Sleep disruptions caused by anxiety or racing thoughts
