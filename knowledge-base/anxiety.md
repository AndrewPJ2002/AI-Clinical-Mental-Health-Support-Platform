# Anxiety & Coping

## Overview
Anxiety is a common emotional response to stress, uncertainty, or overwhelming situations. While occasional anxiety is normal, persistent worry can interfere with daily life. This guide provides practical coping strategies that patients can use to manage anxious thoughts and physical symptoms.

## Key Strategies
- **Breathing exercises:** Slow, deep breathing helps calm the nervous system.
- **Grounding techniques:** Focus on physical sensations (touch, sound, temperature) to stay present.
- **Thought reframing:** Challenge negative or catastrophic thoughts by asking what evidence supports them.
- **Routine building:** Consistent sleep, meals, and activity reduce emotional volatility.
- **Movement:** Light exercise, stretching, or walking can reduce tension.

## When Anxiety Feels Overwhelming
- Persistent worry that disrupts sleep or concentration  
- Physical symptoms such as chest tightness, shaking, or nausea  
- Avoiding daily tasks due to fear or panic  
- Feeling unable to control racing thoughts  

If these symptoms escalate, professional support may be needed.
