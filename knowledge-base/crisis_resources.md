# Crisis Resources

## Overview
During moments of intense distress, immediate support is essential. This guide provides general crisis response steps and resource information for individuals experiencing severe emotional or psychological difficulty.

## Crisis Response Steps
- **Ensure safety:** Move to a safe environment away from harm.
- **Reach out:** Contact a trusted friend, family member, or professional.
- **Use grounding techniques:** Slow breathing, sensory focus, or simple physical actions.
- **Avoid isolation:** Stay connected until the moment of crisis passes.

## When Immediate Help Is Needed
- Thoughts of self-harm or suicide  
- Feeling unable to stay safe  
- Severe panic or emotional breakdown  
- Loss of control over actions or impulses  

## Crisis Resources
- Local emergency services  
- Crisis hotlines  
- Mental health urgent care centers  
- Supportive contacts (friends, family, professionals)

## Additional Notes
This content is for demonstration and educational purposes only. In real deployments, crisis resources must be replaced with verified, location-specific information.
