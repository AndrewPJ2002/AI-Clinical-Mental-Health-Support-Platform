# Healthy Relationships

## Overview
Healthy relationships are built on communication, trust, boundaries, and mutual respect. This guide highlights key principles that support emotional well-being in personal and professional relationships.

## Key Strategies
- **Clear communication:** Express needs and feelings openly and respectfully.
- **Boundary setting:** Identify personal limits and communicate them clearly.
- **Active listening:** Focus on understanding rather than responding.
- **Conflict resolution:** Address issues calmly and work toward shared solutions.
- **Mutual support:** Encourage each other’s goals, growth, and emotional needs.

## Signs of Unhealthy Patterns
- Frequent misunderstandings or unresolved conflicts  
- Feeling drained, anxious, or unsafe  
- Lack of respect for boundaries  
- Manipulation, guilt-tripping, or controlling behavior
